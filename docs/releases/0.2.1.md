Tessera 0.2.1 fixes terminal closing and workspace creation.

- **Close terminal:** Cmd+W replaces Cmd+Shift+W. Confirming stops the shell; closing the last pane removes its workspace. Split panes retain their surviving layout and focus, and workspace tab selection stays valid.
- **Workspace dialog:** Enter submits the focused directory or filter before the Overview or terminal handles it. Invalid directories show an error inside the dialog and remain editable.
- **Retained history:** closed terminals become disconnected immediately. Their history remains available, with clear wording when the workspace has been removed.

Download `Tessera-arm64.dmg` for Apple Silicon or `Tessera-x86_64.dmg` for Intel. Quit Tessera, open the DMG, and drag Tessera to Applications to replace the previous version.
